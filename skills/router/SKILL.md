Pick the line that fits, then follow that skill's steps. After every edit run verify-your-edit, then give the user explain-what-you-did.

- tighten-talking-video: clean up talking footage, pauses and fillers in one pass
- remove-silences: only cut silent pauses or dead air, or tune the pause length
- remove-filler-words: take out um, uh and other filler words
- review-false-starts-and-retakes: check and cut restarted sentences and repeated takes
- cut-to-length: make it shorter or fit a target such as 30 seconds
- keep-the-best-parts: highlight reel, teaser or recap of the best moments
- remove-a-section: delete a named stretch, the intro, the ending or a clip
- add-title: show a title, heading, opening text or a name as text
- add-lower-third: speaker name and role in the lower third
- add-callout: highlight a key number, step or point with a note
- add-end-card-cta: closing call to action or fade-out at the end
- change-format-for-platform: vertical, square or widescreen; TikTok, Reels, Shorts, YouTube
- export-the-video: render and save an MP4, only when asked
- undo-and-versions: undo, redo, go back, checkpoints; use before big edits
- verify-your-edit: check the project after an edit with read-only commands
- handle-ambiguous-requests: vague request or missing detail; ask one question or pick a default
- explain-what-you-did: short honest summary of what changed
- add-music-zoom-or-logo: background music or sound effect from a file, zoom or punch-in, logo or watermark
- handle-unsupported-requests: captions, B-roll, speed, filters, music library; also volume and fades
- tutorial-video-structure: how-to or teaching video with steps
- explainer-video-structure: explainer or pitch with hook, points and call to action
- podcast-clip: short clip from a long podcast or conversation
- product-demo-or-screen-recording: screen recording or software demo cleanup and labels
- short-form-vertical-hook: vertical short with a strong first seconds
- interview-with-lower-thirds: interview or testimonial with names for each speaker
